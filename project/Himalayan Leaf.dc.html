Grown where the cloud sits all morning

Ilam sits on the far eastern ridge of Nepal, on the same soil and the same weather as Darjeeling across the border. Our lots come from smallholder gardens between 1,400 and 2,100 metres, plucked by hand and processed orthodox — rolled, not cut.

Altitude slows the bush down. Slower growth is why these leaves carry the aromatics buyers come to Ilam for.
